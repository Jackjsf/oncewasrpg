About OnceWas RPG

Welcome to OnceWas, a unique tabletop RPG experience where your adventures don't end at the table.

Created by Jack Frank, OnceWas RPG bridges the gap between gaming and literature. Each adventure module you play serves as the opening chapter for a companion novel in The Odd Company Chronicles series.

Using simplified d20 mechanics compatible with D&D and OSR games, OnceWas offers a streamlined system that lets you focus on the story. Whether you're exploring the haunted forests of Silvandor or delving into ancient dungeons, your tabletop experiences come alive in the pages of epic fantasy novels.

Play the adventure. Read the story. Experience both.
